Right-click a door or window block to open or close it.
Right-click a bed at night (or in rain) to sleep until morning.
Right-click a sign to read or write on it. Right-click the glowing runes in the stone ruin (purple dot on the map) to solve the puzzle.
Phone or tablet: joystick to move, drag the screen to look, round buttons to mine, place, jump, use, build and open the bag
Villagers follow a daily routine: they work in the day, chat at the market in the evening, go home, and sleep in their beds at night. The map at top-left shows you (red), villagers (yellow) and animals (white). The game autosaves every 20 seconds.
